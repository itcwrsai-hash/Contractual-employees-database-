EPF is calculated on basic wages (not on other allowances), rounded to the nearest rupee. ESI coverage follows the ESI Act: an employee is covered when their wages for a month, leaving out overtime, are within the ceiling, and then stays covered until the end of that contribution period (April–September or October–March) even if wages later go above it. The ESI contribution is on all gross earnings including overtime, rounded up to the next rupee.
